The project
Every cup can open a conversation, reveal a new origin, introduce a new producer and, perhaps, begin a lasting relationship.

The Taste of the Harvest is an initiative created to bring Brazilian specialty coffees closer to the people who appreciate and cherish them. We believe that meaningful connections are built through experiences, and coffee has a unique way of bringing people together.

The coffees presented in this edition are a reflection of that diversity. They come from different regions and producers across Brazil, each with its own characteristics, history and way of expressing the richness of our coffee-growing landscapes. Bringing these coffees to Japan is an opportunity for us to share not only their quality, but also the people, places and work behind each lot.

This long-standing connection would not be possible without the continued support and collaboration of our partners in Japan. To all the partners and coffee professionals joining us for this experience, thank you for being part of this journey and for taking the time to discover these coffees and the stories behind them.

We hope this selection brings you closer to Brazil and inspires new connections, discoveries and possibilities.

Brazil Specialty Coffee Association (BSCA)

All coffees featured in this selection are certified by BSCA. Buyers who acquire a lot can request the respective Quality Seal, recognizing the coffee’s certified quality and origin.
